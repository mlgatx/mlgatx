A Word to Texas Workers from the MLGA

October 29, 2026 - L. Kynes
Pamphlet

We in the Maoist Labor Group of Austin, alongside other sincere Maoists and Maoist groupings across the country, want to help build back up worker organization and militancy here in America. With unionization at an all time low, especially here in Texas where it sits at around 4 percent in private industry, one can clearly see how dire real change is needed. Of course, and to be absolutely clear, we will never shy away from being explicit with the broader conceptions we posses. We believe in the revolutionary victory of the overthrow of capitalism, which requires the leadership of the working class and its independent political party, and the struggle towards communism. Thus, it is nothing short of obvious that we differ with many on this point, like the union officialdom (e.g. Teamsters, etc.) and other apolitical labor organizing groups, but it is a point we shall never surrender in disseminating, promoting and emphasizing to American workers. These differences notwithstanding, we still aim to work with anyone who seeks to organize the working class into a single and united fighting force, so that, as in the logistics industry for example, a struggle towards higher wages and better working conditions can be secured. Moreover, the current reality of labor regulations, of the NLRB, the Fair Labor Standards Act, among others, is due solely to the victories won by the working class through the rich history of class struggle in this country. It is high time we bring more demands to the table and force concessions from the capitalist class’ hands once again.

On this note, it is important to briefly comment on the fact that there exists in our country many Maoists who think this task of practical work, of the basic organization of the working class and of agitation, as minor or even beneath them. They are more intellectually preoccupied with the isolated study of Marxist theory or of the growth of their own tiny formations at the expense of the development of the movement beyond these narrow bounds. Again, our goal, which is the historical material mission of the working masses, is to upend the system of wage-slavery, of exploitation, and trek a path towards the construction of a new world. However, if concerns in the present are not understood, if the demands and ideas of the workers are not met, then no kind of movement will ever be able to develop. We have written extensively on these weaknesses and thus encourage those interested to read our work to better understand the struggle within the Maoist movement itself (as well as the works of those we have criticized to come to your own conclusions).

Lastly, we understand how Maoism may seem very foreign to your immediate, economic needs. There are several reasons for this. Nevertheless, especially in relation to the above, we square the blame solely on ourselves in hesitating in our work, in having become lethargic and riddled with dogmatic and sectarian tendencies for the better part of half a century. In the continuation of our efforts, the consciousness of the workers of which we seek to organize, which make up the vast super-majority of those living in America, must always be raised and developed alongside any kind of theory, of any kind of strategy, that we conjure. Therefore, we encourage workers who read this pamphlet to think critically about its content, about the information and ideas contained in it, and to always scrutinize such things carefully. Without your input, without your clarifications and criticism, there is only blunder. In a word: we must be better, history necessitates it.
